# Live 11 — Three lines before you leave

**Slot:** 02:55 · Ch11 Close (Q&A) · **Time:** 2 min · **Needs:** nothing — paper only
**Format:** silent, individual, not shared

---

> **The last block is 5 minutes for Ch9 + Ch10 + Ch11.** Run **one** of Live 9, 10 or 11 —
> not all three. This one is the safest close if you are out of time or the room is tired.

## Do this now

Two minutes, no talking, nobody collects these:

1. **One thing you will try this week** — a specific command, technique, or conversation.
2. **One open question** you still have.
3. **One thing that surprised you** today.

---

## What you should get

Number 1 is the one that matters, and it is worth saying why out loud: the gap between this
workshop and a changed team is a single concrete action taken within a week. "Run the
offline healer on our own page objects" beats "adopt agentic testing."

Number 2 is your feedback loop — invite them to send those questions afterwards, and answer
them. Number 3 tells you which chapter actually landed.

---

## Instructor cue

- Actually keep the room silent for the full two minutes. It feels long; it is the only
  reflection time they get.
- Offer to take question 2 by email or Slack, and mean it.
- **Got more time?** The chapter's three games run 8–10 min each and are better than a
  lecture: [Locator Quiz](../games/locator-quiz.md) · [Fix the Broken Test](../games/fix-the-test.md) · [Selector Bingo](../games/selector-bingo.md).
